# Original Eros audio

These instrumental, ambience and sound-effect recordings were synthesized
specifically for Eros by `tools/build_original_audio.py`. They contain no external
sample recordings, sampled compositions, generative audio service output or other
third-party audio material. No additional third-party asset license is required.

The project retains its normal ownership and licensing of these original assets.
Kenney audio and its CC0 license files are stored separately in `../kenney/`.
