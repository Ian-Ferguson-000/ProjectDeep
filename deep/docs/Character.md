# Character Designs

## Demo Class: Fighter
The Fighter is the only playable class in the starter demo. The class should be direct, readable, and tactical: move on the board, choose engagements, block or brace when possible, and use a clear weapon special.

## Baseline Stats
- Max health: 12
- Movement: one grid tile per player action
- Basic interaction: adjacent tiles
- Resource focus: health, block stacks, gold, keys, and potions

## Combat Role
The Fighter survives by choosing good positions. Sword and Shield is defensive, Greatsword is aggressive, and Spear and Shield rewards holding a line.

## Future Classes
Knight, Berserker, Duelist, and Warlord are future Fighter branches or unlockable archetypes after boss defeats.
