# Character Designs

## Demo Classes: Fighter & Mage
The Fighter and Mage are the only playable classes in the starter demo. The classes should be direct, readable, and tactical: move on the board, choose engagements, block or brace when possible, and use a clear weapon/spell special.

## Baseline Stats
- Max health: 12
- Movement: one grid tile per player action
- Basic interaction: adjacent tiles
- Resource focus: health, block stacks, gold, keys, and potions

## Combat Role - Fighter
The Fighter survives by choosing good positions. Sword and Shield is defensive, Greatsword is aggressive, and Spear and Shield rewards holding a line.

## Combat Role - Mage
The Mage survives by strategically using spells to damage enemies from a distance. Magic missile + Shield spell are a good balance of offensive defensive, Fireball + Fire shield spell are great for dishing out wide spread damage and retaliatory offense, Lightning bolt + Flash step spells allow for long range aoe and high mobility

## Future Classes
Knight, Berserker, Duelist, and Warlord are future Fighter branches or unlockable archetypes after boss defeats. Wizard, Pyromancer, Hypermage, and Sorcerer are future mage branches.
