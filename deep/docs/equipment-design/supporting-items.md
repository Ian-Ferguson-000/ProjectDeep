# Supporting item catalogue

[Overview and shared limits](README.md) · [Existing-content audit](existing-content-audit.md) · [Review workbook](review-workbook.md)

**48 recommended entries: six per class plus 12 shared. Twelve reserve entries follow.** All are unapproved proposals. Retained names count here once; this is not 48 additions on top of the old pool. Values are illustrative. All items last the expedition, occupy one personal slot, and are unique/nonstacking. Replacing one does not reset its counters. Unless stated otherwise, only the owner's direct kit actions trigger effects; all added damage is secondary under the overview rules.

**Acquisition defaults:** Common = ordinary chest/room reward or expedition merchant; Uncommon = elite/chest reward or merchant; Rare = elite/boss reward. Every class-focused item also uses capability filtering, including for other classes. Flavor sources below are preferred pool weights, not mandatory new rooms. Costs are production complexity: Low = existing event/condition; Medium = new event or visible counter; High = ownership/geometry subsystem. These are estimates, not implementation claims.

## Warrior-focused recommendations

<a id="itm-war-01"></a>
### ITM-WAR-01 — Runebound Bracer

**Uncommon · Low · Revised existing identity.** After a successful timed defense prevents enemy damage, the next direct Basic within 3s gains a 25% wider arc or line; a single-target projectile instead gains a small impact splash. Consume once on activation, hit or miss; 4s internal cooldown. Encourage answering a defended attack rather than holding the benefit forever. Fits standard Warrior, Greatsword, Fox Teeth, and other reactive kits. Pair with Mirror of the Second Guard for an offensive answer, but the relic's secondary return cannot consume or trigger this item. **Source:** martial elite caches. Bosses keep normal hit limits.

<a id="itm-war-02"></a>
### ITM-WAR-02 — Redstep Spurs

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** After moving at least medium distance through ordinary movement or a movement ability during combat, the next melee Basic within 2s can be aimed during its first 0.15s of windup. It does not cancel recovery, teleport the attack, or increase damage. Once per 3s. Makes an approach easier to commit to without converting mobility into raw power. Fits Charge, Banner, Fox Teeth, and chain play; not ranged casts. **Source:** martial/road rewards. Pair with Pilgrim Bell for a mobile sequence. Overlong knockback still spoils reach; no resource from merely running in circles.

<a id="itm-war-03"></a>
### ITM-WAR-03 — Veteran's Whetstone

**Common · Medium · Revised existing identity.** Two consecutive direct melee Basics against the same target make the next Basic against it apply a 2s, 15% armor-mitigation reduction, then restart the count. Misses or switching targets reset progress. Does not reduce invulnerability, boss damage caps, or resistance below zero. Encourages finishing a duel. Fits standard Warrior, Greatsword, Fox Teeth, and Tank; weak for frequent target switching. **Source:** martial rewards/merchant. Pair with Hunter's Star for committed single-target play. Multiple copies and allied applications refresh one debuff rather than stack it.

<a id="itm-war-04"></a>
### ITM-WAR-04 — Giant-Bane Grip

**Rare · Medium · Revised existing identity.** A heavy direct Special hitting an elite/boss also applies a 2s visible Breach. The next direct Basic by the owner against that target ignores 15% of ordinary mitigation and consumes Breach. One Breach at a time, 5s internal cooldown. Rewards following a punish with a lighter attack rather than repeating only the finisher. Fits all Warrior kits, Tank, and melee Rogue Specials; does not bypass phase shields. **Source:** elite/boss martial pools. Pair with Worldbreaker Gauntlet; both mitigation reductions share a 25% reduction ceiling rather than multiplying toward zero defense.

<a id="itm-war-05"></a>
### ITM-WAR-05 — Spearhead Ferrule

**Uncommon · Medium · New.** A direct line attack connecting in its outer quarter leaves a 2s aiming guide to that target. The next line attack following that guide widens 20%, then consumes it. One guide, 3s internal cooldown; no homing. Rewards measured distance. Fits Borderkeeper, chain throws, piercing Rogue attacks, and golem lines where a command explicitly qualifies; not circles or cones. **Source:** martial elite rewards. Pair with Warden's Seal on Borderkeeper for lane control. Boss movement can invalidate the angle; an off-angle shot consumes the guide without benefit, preserving precision.

<a id="itm-war-06"></a>
### ITM-WAR-06 — Rally Pennant

**Uncommon · Medium · New.** After landing a Basic while inside an owned field, gain a 2s buffer that preserves that field's personal movement-speed or defensive benefit for 1s after leaving. Once per 5s; does not preserve damage pulses, healing, or the field itself. Encourages advancing from prepared ground. Fits Banner, Censer, and Citadel; no field means ineligible. **Source:** martial/support elite pools. Pair with Pilgrim Bell to leave a rally zone safely. Only the item owner receives the buffer; allied aura beneficiaries cannot carry copies across the room. No extension after kit swapping.

## Mage-focused recommendations

<a id="itm-mag-01"></a>
### ITM-MAG-01 — Embershard Focus

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** The first direct fire hit on a nonburning enemy extends that application of Burn by 1s. Subsequent hits only refresh the ordinary duration; this bonus cannot accumulate on one target. Encourages starting fires across several foes. Fits Pyromancy and Furnace Plate only when an action actually applies Burn; fire damage alone is insufficient. **Source:** fire-themed rooms/merchant. Pair with Crown of the First Flame for deliberate detonation. Standard Mage's currently arcane Fireball is ineligible. Burn ticks never generate Focus, and a consumed Burn loses the extension completely.

<a id="itm-mag-02"></a>
### ITM-MAG-02 — Flameheart Talisman

**Uncommon · Medium · Revised existing identity.** A direct fire action hitting an enemy within close range leaves a small 1s warm foothold at the owner's feet. While on it, reduce enemy forced movement by half; 4s internal cooldown. No damage, healing, or immunity to hazards. Makes close Pyromancy/Furnace play deliberate rather than granting a blanket damage bonus. **Source:** fire elites. Pair with Briar Seed to hold a retaliatory position. A foothold counts as an item field, not a kit field for other item triggers, preventing self-sustaining field chains.

<a id="itm-mag-03"></a>
### ITM-MAG-03 — Rime Spindle

**Uncommon · Medium · New.** Consuming three owned Chill stacks with a direct ability leaves one fragile icy line behind the target for 2s. The next ordinary enemy crossing it is Slowed; the line then vanishes. Against a lone boss it instead lengthens the owner's next Chill application by 1s, once per 5s. No additional hard freeze. Fits Winterglass and future explicit Chill kits only. **Source:** control-themed elites. Pair with Starfallen Sigil for fracture cadence; its echo never consumes Chill again. Gives Fracture a repositioning consequence, not infinite crowd control.

<a id="itm-mag-04"></a>
### ITM-MAG-04 — Stormglass Bead

**Common · Medium · Revised existing identity; still a proposal.** After movement, the next direct lightning chain within 2s gains 20% bounce reach. It does not add targets, remove damage falloff, or require a conductor. Once per 3s; activation consumes the window. Encourages relocating a circuit instead of turret play. Fits Stormbringer's Grimoire, not every electrical-looking VFX. **Source:** arcane rewards/merchant. Pair with Pilgrim Bell; their mobility rewards operate independently. A lone boss is never its own bounce target. No arc can route through enemy projectiles or allied characters.

<a id="itm-mag-05"></a>
### ITM-MAG-05 — Aether Prism

**Uncommon · Medium · Revised existing identity.** Every third direct arcane projectile cast can pass through its first enemy, but deals 25% less direct damage on that cast. If already piercing, increase its range 15% instead. Counter advances on successful casts, not secondary bolts; one benefit across a multi-projectile cast. Creates a spacing-versus-concentration choice. Fits standard Mage, Gravity, and Mirrorbound. **Source:** arcane caches. Pair with Starfallen Sigil, whose echo copies the resolved attack without advancing the counter. Not compatible with fire/cold/lightning-only kits unless they acquire an actual arcane projectile.

<a id="itm-mag-06"></a>
### ITM-MAG-06 — Chalk Rune Tablet

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** Successfully defending against an enemy attack draws a rune for 3s. The next directly aimed spell Special cast while standing on it may be re-aimed during its first 0.2s of windup. Rune consumed on activation; 5s internal cooldown. Rewards planning a cast after danger rather than automatic damage. Fits projectile, cone, or placed spell Specials, including all Mage recommendations. **Source:** arcane rewards/merchant. Pair with Chronomancer's Pin for a controlled defense-to-offense rhythm. Cannot move an already spawned projectile or repeatedly relocate a delayed detonation.

## Healer-focused recommendations

<a id="itm-hea-01"></a>
### ITM-HEA-01 — Healer's Brooch

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** A healing potion also clears self Slow and the remaining duration of one Poison. Actual cleansing grants 1 class resource, once per encounter; drinking an unnecessary potion grants none. The item does not create potions or add healing. Fits any class, especially Censer or low-mobility Tank. **Source:** support rewards/merchant. Pair with Apothecary's Cup to improve a scarce consumable's timing. Cleansed dot damage is not “prevented damage” for reflection, retaliation, or additional healing; cleansing two statuses is still one resource award.

<a id="itm-hea-02"></a>
### ITM-HEA-02 — Saint's Phial

**Uncommon · Medium · Revised existing identity.** After a kit action restores actual lost health, preserve a visual trace of the restored amount for 2s. The next enemy hit during that time cannot push the owner; the trace then expires. No added healing or damage reduction, 5s internal cooldown. Supports maintaining position after recovery. Fits Sunwood, Thorn-Mercy, Censer, and Pilgrim; Last Rites qualifies only if another source restores health, not when debt is canceled. **Source:** support elites. Pair with Tide Pearl. Overheal, barrier creation, and damage deferral do not count as restoration.

<a id="itm-hea-03"></a>
### ITM-HEA-03 — Sunward Periapt

**Uncommon · Medium · Revised existing identity.** Once per encounter, healing that would exceed full health becomes a 10%-max-health barrier lasting 3s, capped by the unused amount. No actual excess means no barrier. Fits any healing-capable kit, potions included; recommended for Healer but valid elsewhere. **Source:** support elites. Pair with Warden's Seal for a single protected reposition. Shares the global barrier cap and highest-value rule; Apothecary's Cup consumes potion overheal first, so the same excess is never converted twice. The standard renewable healing dash cannot rearm this encounter use.

<a id="itm-hea-04"></a>
### ITM-HEA-04 — Pilgrim's Greaves

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** After actual health restoration from a kit action, the next movement action within 3s travels 15% farther, without extending invulnerability. Once per 5s. Rewards using restoration to change position. Fits Sunwood and Pilgrim naturally; Thorn-Mercy can heal then retreat. **Source:** support/road rewards. Pair with Pilgrim Bell; distance changes do not multiply Bell's benefit. Excess distance is collision-clamped and never crosses a forbidden gap. Items or relics that merely prevent/defer damage do not activate the greaves; no heal means no extra range.

<a id="itm-hea-05"></a>
### ITM-HEA-05 — Dawn Wick

**Common · Low · New.** The first direct radiant Basic after a successful Special leaves a 2s target marker. Your next direct Basic can hit that target through one nonblocking decorative prop, not a wall or closed door; consumes marker on cast. One marker, 4s internal cooldown. Helps a support caster maintain damage through clutter. Fits all Healer kits with radiant Basics. **Source:** support rewards/merchant. Pair with Hunter's Star for priority pressure. No damage increase against an unobstructed boss; still offers clarity about the next target. Never bypasses line-of-sight gates.

<a id="itm-hea-06"></a>
### ITM-HEA-06 — Mercy Thread

**Rare · Medium · New.** A direct enemy hit that consumes an owned healing-related mark extends one existing self barrier by 1s, once per 5s; no barrier means a 2s window to apply that extension to the next one. Does not create barrier strength. Fits Thorn-Mercy Seeds and Censer Ash; ordinary Poison consumption is not healing-related. **Source:** support boss/elite pool. Pair with Sunward Periapt for a protected harvest. This modifier improves a loop already supplied by the kit; without a barrier it is a weaker pick, and reward matching must recognize that condition.

## Tank-focused recommendations

<a id="itm-tan-01"></a>
### ITM-TAN-01 — Soldier's Buckle

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** After a timed Defensive action prevents enemy damage, the owner resists forced movement for 1s. Once per 3s; does not negate ordinary movement inputs or extend the defense window. Choose it to remain in range for a counter. Fits Tank, Warrior, and other mitigation kits; pure decoys without prevention do not qualify. **Source:** martial rewards/merchant. Pair with Briar Seed for close retaliation. It grants no flat mitigation and cannot convert blocked damage into more blocked hits. Boss arena knockback explicitly marked unavoidable remains unavoidable.

<a id="itm-tan-02"></a>
### ITM-TAN-02 — Ironroot Ring

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** After standing within a close-radius area for 1.5s during combat, reduce ordinary forced-movement distance by 50%. Walking outside the area or using movement ends it. No damage reduction or resource. Encourages holding a planned location but not idling for power. Fits Iron Porter, Citadel, and stationary ranged builds. **Source:** defense rewards/merchant. Pair with Heartwood Crown. Multiple anti-knockback effects use the strongest reduction rather than multiplying; hazards that require movement still force the player to leave.

<a id="itm-tan-03"></a>
### ITM-TAN-03 — Brambleguard Clasp

**Uncommon · Low · Revised existing identity.** A successful defense against a close melee enemy leaves a thorn mark for 2s. The next direct Basic against that attacker adds a small physical secondary hit, then consumes the mark. One mark, 4s internal cooldown. Rewards a targeted counter rather than passive thorns. Fits Tank and Warrior; also Evade when an attacker is known. **Source:** defense elites. Pair with Worldbreaker Gauntlet, but the thorn hit cannot apply its shred or generate a second mark. A ranged attacker outside close range grants no thorn mark.

<a id="itm-tan-04"></a>
### ITM-TAN-04 — Oathlink

**Uncommon · Medium · New.** After the owner takes a direct enemy hit while a living owned summon or deployed ally is within close range, the next Defensive action within 3s gains 0.2s to its activation window. Once per 6s; no extra invulnerability and no larger reduction. With no ally or summon, a direct hit from a kit-marked enemy qualifies instead. Fits Oathwall, Mirror Aegis when marking via another effect, and Summoner. **Source:** defense/support elites. Pair with Standard of Succession. Intercepted damage, debt repayment, dots, and self damage cannot trigger it.

<a id="itm-tan-05"></a>
### ITM-TAN-05 — Porter's Strap

**Uncommon · Medium · New.** Recalling or relocating an owned non-damaging cover field grants 15% walking speed for 1.5s. Once per 5s; no movement-CD reduction. Fits Iron Porter, Winterglass only if a later action actually relocates its wall, and any explicitly compatible portable cover. Banner is an aura, not cover. **Source:** defense elites. Pair with Pilgrim Bell to change a firing lane. Benefits end on party/kit switch; field expiry, overwrite, and removal do not count as recall. Deliberately niche: filter it out when the current kit has no qualifying action.

<a id="itm-tan-06"></a>
### ITM-TAN-06 — Boiler Rivet

**Rare · Medium · New.** Consuming three Heat with Open the Vents leaves one empty Heat slot “warm” for 2s. The first direct Basic hit during that time restores one Heat, once per Special. No Resolve award. Encourages immediately returning to melee after a full vent. Fits Furnace Plate only in the current catalogue. **Source:** fire/defense elite and boss pools. Pair with Crown of the First Flame; relic echoes cannot consume or restore Heat. A full-cost miss consumes Heat but leaves no warm slot, so aim still matters. Highly specific offers require the corresponding kit.

## Rogue-focused recommendations

<a id="itm-rog-01"></a>
### ITM-ROG-01 — Duelist Gloves

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** The first direct melee hit on an isolated enemy each 4s grants a 2s mark. The next melee Basic on that enemy has 20% extra reach, then consumes the mark. Isolated means no other hostile within close range at initial hit. Fits Rogue and Warrior duels; not projectile-only kits. **Source:** finesse rewards/merchant. Pair with Hunter's Star, which uses its own distinct focus rule. Bosses can qualify normally. Does not increase dash distance or allow attacks through walls; nearby boss adds can remove the opportunity.

<a id="itm-rog-02"></a>
### ITM-ROG-02 — Moonleaf Cloak

**Uncommon · Low · Revised existing identity.** Gaining kit-authored Hidden extends it by 0.25s once, up to a total 1s. Hidden still ends on the same attack/reveal conditions. Once per 4s; never refresh through item effects. Gives a little more time to choose an angle. Fits standard Rogue; kits without Hidden are ineligible even if their theme is shadowy. **Source:** finesse elites. Pair with Hunter's Star for deliberate ambush. Does not extend invulnerability, erase telegraphed attacks, or make enemies forget their current action. No benefit to Fox Teeth or Moon Stiletto as written.

<a id="itm-rog-03"></a>
### ITM-ROG-03 — Venom Glass

**Uncommon · Medium · New.** A direct Poison-applying hit on an already Poisoned target creates a one-use 2s window: moving at least close distance before the next hit preserves that Poison's remaining duration during movement, maximum 0.75s. Once per 5s per owner. Encourages a retreat before harvesting. Fits Viper and future actual Poison kits. **Source:** finesse/alchemical elites. Pair with Briar Seed for a controlled retreat. No added stacks, tick rate, resource, or multiplied harvest damage. Boss poison immunity gives no window; direct damage remains the kit's fallback.

<a id="itm-rog-04"></a>
### ITM-ROG-04 — Tripwire Spool

**Rare · Medium · New.** A placed kit trap can trigger from 20% farther along its forward-facing edge, but not its rear. The visible trigger outline updates before arming. No additional damage or extra charge. Fits Trapper and other explicit one-shot ground traps; persistent fire patches and stone plates are not traps. **Source:** finesse elite/boss rewards. Pair with Warden's Seal to create a prepared defensive lane. Bosses can trigger within the expanded outline without being rooted. Trigger expansion cannot extend through walls, closed doors, or into an unengaged room.

<a id="itm-rog-05"></a>
### ITM-ROG-05 — Fletching Comb

**Common · Low · New.** After a direct arrow or bolt travels at least medium distance and hits an enemy, the next arrow/bolt within 2s has 15% faster travel speed. Once per 3s; not faster attack or reload speed. Rewards holding ranged space. Fits Trapper and Last Whisper; magic projectiles are excluded. **Source:** finesse rewards/merchant. Pair with Hunter's Star. All projectiles from one activation share one award; reflected arrows do not qualify. Misses can consume the prepared shot, so the item improves feel without automatically correcting aim.

<a id="itm-rog-06"></a>
### ITM-ROG-06 — Escape Knot

**Uncommon · Low · New.** Using a movement action while an enemy is within close range grants 20% walking speed for 1s after landing. Once per 4s; no extra protection or attack speed. Rewards escaping pressure rather than spamming movement across empty rooms. Fits every class, weighted toward Rogue. **Source:** finesse elites. Pair with Chronomancer's Pin to alternate escape and measured offense. Enemies spawned by the owner do not qualify; proximity is checked at departure. Does not keep stacking with other walking-speed buffs—use the strongest.

## Summoner-focused recommendations

<a id="itm-sum-01"></a>
### ITM-SUM-01 — Command Whistle

**Common · Medium · New.** After a commanded summon attack actually hits its designated enemy, the next command issued within 2s allows a 20% wider target-selection cone. Once per 3s across all summons. No extra attack, damage, or Bond. Fits every Summoner kit and future explicit command kits. **Source:** companion rewards/merchant. Pair with Twin Command Seal for reliable target changes. Repeatedly marking a target in the standard kit does not qualify until a wolf attack connects. A failed or positional command consumes the prepared targeting benefit without granting resource.

<a id="itm-sum-02"></a>
### ITM-SUM-02 — Bond Collar

**Common · Medium · New.** If a kit Defensive action receives its stronger version because an owned companion is nearby or available, preserve that stronger reduction for the next 0.25s even if the companion moves away. Once per 4s; never extend the defense's total life or protection count. Fits wolf Cover, Pack rearguard, golem Shelter, moth reserve, and stag Shelter. **Source:** companion rewards/merchant. Pair with Pilgrim Bell for repositioning together. Multiple pets do not multiply the buffer. It improves reliability at a boundary rather than making summons invulnerable.

<a id="itm-sum-03"></a>
### ITM-SUM-03 — Pack Harness

**Uncommon · High · New.** If two distinct owned mobile companions hit the same commanded target within 1s, their next reposition order uses separated safe destinations instead of overlapping. Lasts 3s, once per 5s. No damage or extra bodies. Fits Pack Call; moths lack ground formation and are ineligible. **Source:** companion elites. Pair with Twin Command Seal. A corridor may force adjacent fallback positions; never teleport through obstacles to satisfy spacing. Improves pincer readability and recovery without making a perfect flank mandatory for baseline damage.

<a id="itm-sum-04"></a>
### ITM-SUM-04 — Clay Seal

**Uncommon · Medium · New.** Once per encounter, when a damageable owned companion drops below half health from enemy damage, grant it a 10%-of-its-max-health barrier for 3s. Shared once across all pets; no heal or resource. Fits golem, hounds, vine, and other actually damageable companions; not the moth choir. **Source:** companion elites. Pair with Warden's Seal, which can protect the owner on the same planned defensive sequence but cannot duplicate this companion barrier. Resummoning or changing kit cannot reset the use; deliberate pet sacrifice is not rewarded.

<a id="itm-sum-05"></a>
### ITM-SUM-05 — Choir Wick

**Rare · Medium · New.** When a moth returns after connecting with an enemy, the next moth dispatched within 1s travels 15% faster outward. Once per 3s across the flock. Does not shorten return time or increase availability. Fits Moth Choir only; offer only with that kit. **Source:** companion elite/boss rewards. Pair with Twin Command Seal for controlled dispatch cadence. A recalled moth that never hit cannot trigger the item. This makes the rhythm more responsive without replacing the finite-flock constraint with unrestricted spell spam.

<a id="itm-sum-06"></a>
### ITM-SUM-06 — Keeper's Leash

**Uncommon · High · New.** Issuing a ground-position command draws a 2s route guide for the owner and companion. The next command can snap to one of two safe nearby offsets of that point, selected by aim. Once per 4s. No teleport or autonomous attack. Fits wolf repositioning, golem orders, and other ground commands; moth targeting has no ground command and is ineligible. **Source:** companion elites. Pair with Wayfinder's Compass to favor deliberate exploration and positioning. Guides cannot reveal unexplored terrain or bypass navigation; a blocked offset falls back to the original legal point.

## Shared recommendations

<a id="itm-shr-01"></a>
### ITM-SHR-01 — Keen Edge Oil

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** First direct damaging Basic against each encounter's first enemy applies a 2s mark; the next Special hitting that enemy ignores 10% ordinary mitigation, then consumes it. Once per encounter. Works with weapon, spell, or explicitly commanded summon Basics so all classes have an opener. **Source:** ordinary rewards/merchant. Pair with Giant-Bane Grip for alternating Basic and Special, respecting the shared 25% mitigation-reduction ceiling. Does not bypass immunity. Keep the oil as an expedition item, not a temporary two-floor stat buff or permanent accessory.

<a id="itm-shr-02"></a>
### ITM-SHR-02 — Quickstep Charm

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** A movement action ending farther from the nearest enemy than it began grants 15% walking speed for 1s. Once per 4s. Fits every kit and rewards a clear retreat rather than any dash. **Source:** ordinary rewards/merchant. Pair with Pilgrim Bell. It competes with Escape Knot for a personal slot; their speed bonuses do not add, so the choice is reliable retreat versus activation under pressure. No trigger when no enemy was present at departure; cannot accelerate empty-room farming.

<a id="itm-shr-03"></a>
### ITM-SHR-03 — Amulet of Vigor

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** Once per encounter, taking a direct enemy hit that crosses below half health grants 1s resistance to ordinary forced movement. No heal, damage reduction, or resource. Helps a player recover control without making low health a damage strategy. **Source:** ordinary rewards/merchant. Fits all classes; pair with Phoenix-Ash Reliquary, whose lethal prevention remains separate. Self damage, debt repayment, and maximum-health changes cannot trigger it. Taking another hit while already below half does not reset the crossing condition.

<a id="itm-shr-04"></a>
### ITM-SHR-04 — Scholar's Lens

**Common · Medium · Revised existing identity.** After a direct action is heavily mitigated by an enemy, show that enemy's active resistance category and remaining visible shield duration for 3s. Once per target per encounter; informational only. Fits all classes, including summon commands. **Source:** ordinary/arcane rewards. Pair with War Scholar's Codex to plan a different action. Never reveals hidden story knowledge or unknown boss phases. A shield without a known duration shows “active” rather than inventing a timer. Requires readable inspection UI, not a covert damage bonus.

<a id="itm-shr-05"></a>
### ITM-SHR-05 — Boots of the Hare

**Uncommon · Low · Revised existing identity.** If at least two enemies are close when a movement action begins, allow a 15% shorter landing by releasing the movement direction early during the first half of travel. Teleports choose the shorter endpoint before activation instead. Once per 4s. Helps avoid overshooting into another pack. **Source:** ordinary elites. Fits all mobility kits; pair with Escape Knot. Does not extend protection or bypass collision. This optional precision aid must remain usable on controller without requiring rapid double inputs.

<a id="itm-shr-06"></a>
### ITM-SHR-06 — Kingsroad Aegis

**Uncommon · Low · Revised existing identity.** The first direct enemy hit each encounter cannot apply Slow or ordinary knockback, but deals its normal damage. A successful full dodge does not spend the protection. Fits every class; choose it for a stable opening. **Source:** defense/ordinary elite rewards. Pair with Keen Edge Oil to complete an opener under pressure. No immunity to roots, scripted arena movement, or damage-over-time after the hit. Unlike Soldier's Buckle it requires no successful defense, but works only once per encounter.

<a id="itm-shr-07"></a>
### ITM-SHR-07 — Sparkwire Wrap

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** The first direct enemy hit after using two different action slots adds one small lightning secondary hit. 5s internal cooldown; the qualifying hit consumes the sequence even on a boss shield. Fits every class, including a commanded summon hit following its owner's two-slot sequence. **Source:** ordinary rewards/merchant. Pair with Unwritten Equation. The spark cannot arc, generate resource, or activate lightning-cast items; it is an item proc, not a Stormbringer spell. Repeatedly pressing a failed action does not build the sequence.

<a id="itm-shr-08"></a>
### ITM-SHR-08 — Mender's Sachet

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** Once per encounter, a direct enemy hit crossing below half health drops a personal recovery mote at that position for 3s. Walking over it heals 5% max health; missing it forfeits the heal. Subject to the shared item/relic healing budget. **Source:** ordinary/support rewards. Fits all classes; pair with Escape Knot to circle back safely. The mote cannot be collected by a summon or another adventurer, teleported to inventory, or respawned by re-entering the room. Creates a spatial recovery choice.

<a id="itm-shr-09"></a>
### ITM-SHR-09 — Windknotted Thread

**Common · Low · Revised existing identity.** After 2s in combat with no enemy within close range, the next Basic has 15% farther reach/range. Consume on activation, then wait another 2s. Does not increase damage, field size, or projectile count. Fits all classes; for commanded summons it extends command selection, not pet attack reach. **Source:** ordinary rewards/merchant. Pair with Fletching Comb for ranged spacing. A close enemy breaks preparation. It supports an opener from safety without letting a melee kit maintain unlimited distant attacks.

<a id="itm-shr-10"></a>
### ITM-SHR-10 — Drillmaster's Whistle

**Uncommon · Medium · Revised existing identity.** After a successful defense against an enemy, the next different action slot within 2s refunds 1 resource if that action actually spent resource and hit an enemy. Once per 8s. Refund never exceeds the cost; no XP generation. Fits all classes with a damaging resource spender; Sunwood's Empower pays out only when its empowered shot later connects within the window. **Source:** ordinary elites. Pair with Chronomancer's Pin, respecting the combined refund limit. Empty casts and repeated standard Summoner marks cannot farm resource.

<a id="itm-shr-11"></a>
### ITM-SHR-11 — Apothecary's Cup

**Uncommon · Medium · Revised existing identity.** Once per encounter, unused healing from a consumed potion becomes a 3s barrier, capped at 10% max health and the actual excess. Potion count is still spent. **Source:** support/ordinary elites. Fits every class; pair with Healer's Brooch. Process this conversion before Sunward Periapt, which receives only any unconverted excess, with one highest-value barrier overall. No refill, resale, or potion duplication. The choice is using a scarce heal for planned protection versus preserving it for injury.

<a id="itm-shr-12"></a>
### ITM-SHR-12 — War Scholar's Codex

**Rare · Medium · Revised existing identity.** Hitting the same enemy with Basic then Special marks it Studied for 3s. The next Defensive action while it remains close gains 0.15s activation window, then consumes Studied. One target, 6s internal cooldown. ## Reserve candidates

Each is a complete substitute, not an assumed expansion. Two are oriented toward each class; capability filtering still applies.

<a id="itm-res-01"></a>
### ITM-RES-01 — Captain's Scabbard

**Warrior · Uncommon · Medium.** Replace Rally Pennant if active field management proves too niche. After two different melee actions hit enemies within 3s, the next movement action leaves a harmless 2s rally marker. Returning to it grants 1 Momentum once, 8s internal cooldown. Only Warrior resource is awarded; no cross-class offer without an explicit adaptation. **Source:** martial elites. Pair with Banner or standard Warrior and Pilgrim Bell. Marker cannot trigger field items; leaving the room erases it. Encourages a return path rather than constant forward charges.

<a id="itm-res-02"></a>
### ITM-RES-02 — Balanced Pommel

**Warrior · Common · Low.** Replace Veteran's Whetstone if mitigation reduction is too opaque. A missed charged melee Basic shortens its recovery by 15%, once per 4s, without refunding resource or dealing damage. Fits Greatsword and other explicit held melee attacks. **Source:** martial rewards/merchant. Pair with Giant-Bane Grip; gives a forgiving setup without rewarding deliberate misses with more damage than hits. No effect on instant Basics, Special windups, canceled attacks, or interruption recovery. Keep the miss sound clear so the player understands why the timing changed.

<a id="itm-res-03"></a>
### ITM-RES-03 — Glass Magus Eye

**Mage · Rare · Medium · Revised existing identity.** Replace Aether Prism for precision casting. Once per 5s; guide consumed on cast. **Source:** arcane boss/elite rewards. Fits Winterglass, Mirrorbound, and standard Mage; pair with Hunter's Star. No immunity bypass or interaction with hidden range-derived penetration. Taking enemy damage breaks the guide, creating a focus-versus-evasion choice.

### ITM-RES-04 — Ashen Bookmark

Pair with Tome of the Pyromancer and Crown of the First Flame. Field echoes do not create bookmarks. Supports alternating placement and shots, while close furnace builds may prefer the recommended talisman. Only one outline survives; overwriting it does not trigger anything.
