# Fighter Gear

The starter demo offers three Fighter gear kits. Each kit should change how the player thinks about positioning.

## Sword and Shield
- Damage: 1
- Defense: can block attacks with limited block stacks
- Special: Charge
- Charge moves forward in a straight line and strikes the first enemy hit, pushing through open space.

## Greatsword
- Damage: 3
- Defense: cannot block
- Special: Sweep Strike
- Sweep Strike attacks every adjacent enemy.

## Spear and Shield
- Damage: 2
- Defense: shield support
- Special: Brace
- Brace prepares a free hit against the next enemy that approaches.

## Demo Necklace
Veteran Necklace is a future non-weapon gear idea. It grants Battle Hardened, preventing critical hits from the front. It is out of scope for the starter demo.

## Future Boss Rewards
- Knight: tank and shield specialist.
- Berserker: two-handed damage and rage.
- Duelist: counterattacks and mobility.
- Warlord: ally buffs and battlecry effects.
