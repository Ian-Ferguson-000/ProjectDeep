# Eros - High Concept

## Pitch
Eros is a turn-based tabletop roguelike where every run begins in a candlelit tavern, descends into dangerous fantasy floors, and ends with either glory or another strange conversation with the bartender who remembers more than he should.

## Player Fantasy
You are an adventurer at the edge of a mythic campaign. The tavern is your sanctuary, your armory, and your confessional. Beyond its door, the world rearranges itself into tactical dungeon boards filled with monsters, traps, hidden caches, and relics worth dying for.

## Core Hook
Death does not erase the adventure. It sends the player back to the tavern with a new rumor, a new bargain, and a sharper understanding of the dungeon below.

## First Demo Promise
The first playable demo should prove the main loop:

1. Start in the tavern.
2. Speak to the bartender.
3. Choose one Fighter gear kit.
4. Enter a seeded forest dungeon.
5. Fight, loot, discover, and survive.
6. Return to the tavern after victory or death.

## Tone
The game begins warm, grounded, and a little funny, then becomes darker and stranger the deeper the party descends. The forest should feel like the first step away from safety: readable, tactical, and dangerous without becoming overwhelming.

## Current Demo Scope
The original single-Fighter prototype has expanded into the persistent roster campaign defined in `RoguelikeGameLoopExpansion.md`. The target remains single-player, 2D, and top-down, with six classes, two combat modes, five regular dungeons, two secret dungeons, permadeath, merchants, and tavern meta-progression. Multiplayer and daily challenges remain outside the demo.
