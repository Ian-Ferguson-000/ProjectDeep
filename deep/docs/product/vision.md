# Eros Product Vision

**Purpose:** Define the durable product promise.  
**Audience:** Entire team.  
**Owner:** Product/design.  
**Status:** Approved baseline.  
**Authority:** Canonical product vision.

Eros is a single-player, 2D tavern-management roguelite in which the player evaluates persistent adventurers, invests in their preparation, directly controls their party in dangerous expeditions, and repeatedly decides whether to extract or risk descending. The tavern is both a business and the campaign’s progression spine.

## Design Pillars

1. **Informed uncertainty:** candidates begin partially understood, but every reveal is truthful and player choices improve information.
2. **Preparation matters:** party composition, actions, equipment, provisions, liabilities, and dungeon knowledge materially change expeditions.
3. **Extraction creates tension:** cleared checkpoints offer a legible choice between secured progress and deeper rewards.
4. **Characters create history:** adventurers accumulate traits, relationships, injuries, achievements, and personal losses.
5. **The tavern expands options:** upgrades improve recruitment, recovery, crafting, information, and content access instead of supplying only linear stats.
6. **Two tactical expressions, one campaign:** Strategy and Slasher share characters, rules, rewards, and consequences while presenting different control models.

The final game supports dozens of classes and destinations. The demo proves the same architecture with deliberately limited authored content; it is not a separate simplified ruleset.
