# Eros Demo Scope

**Purpose:** Define the content-limited, systems-complete demo.  
**Audience:** Product, production, design, and engineering.  
**Owner:** Production/design.  
**Status:** Approved target.  
**Authority:** Canonical demo boundary.

## Content Profile

- Six classes: Warrior, Mage, Tank, Rogue, Healer, Summoner.
- Five regular destinations: Verdant Forest, Ashen Farmstead, Stone Crypt, Sunken Mine, Ember Foundry.
- Two secret destinations: Moonlit Grove and Abyssal Archive.
- Six merchants: Mara Vell plus one associated with each regular dungeon.
- Two combat modes in every destination: Strategy and Slasher.
- Up to three independent campaign save slots.

## Required Core Systems

The demo is not complete until it includes tavern service income; candidate assessment and recruitment; visible contracts and tabs; party/loadout/provision preparation; fatigue, stress, Downed, stabilization, injury, recovery, and permanent death; secured/unsecured rewards; extraction, flee, wipe, rescue, and victory settlement; equipment ownership, durability, storage, repair, crafting, and assignment; tavern upgrades, reputation, Favor, relationships, deterministic generation, accessibility settings, telemetry, and validation tools.

## Tutorial

The tutorial controls a preset 3-HP Warrior in Verdant Forest and teaches both combat and checkpoint risk. It may end in victory or unresolved Downed/death. On victory, the bankrupt former keeper exchanges the tavern for the boss haul; on death, ownership transfers through inheritance/scavenging. Dialogue and a cosmetic keepsake differ, but both branches converge on identical resources, candidates, equipment, unlocks, and difficulty.

## Explicitly Post-Demo

Aging and descendants, simultaneous expedition teams, seasonal simulation, asynchronous expeditions, decoration placement, daily challenges, and endless ascension must not block demo completion.
