# Relationships and Events

**Purpose:** Define authored social change and event behavior.  
**Audience:** Narrative, systems, and content teams.  
**Owner:** Narrative/systems design.  
**Status:** Canonical demo requirements.  
**Authority:** Relationships and event consequences.

Relationships change through tagged authored outcomes such as shared success, rescue, compatible preferences, conflict, abandonment, mentorship, and rivalry. They do not drift randomly without a visible cause. Threshold rewards favor dialogue, combo actions, recovery options, rescue behavior, and event choices over large raw-stat bonuses.

Events declare requirements, revealed information, choices, checks, outcomes, relationship tags, costs, and deterministic random stream. Any comparison or assessment result must be truthful. Personal quests, scars, accomplishments, tavern roles, and retirement hooks accumulate history; descendants and household simulation remain post-demo.
