# Core Cast Briefs

## The Keeper

The player is an authored but unnamed Keeper from the first frame. The Keeper owns responsibility, not heroic destiny: contracts, hospitality, preparation, credit, memorials, and sanctuary law. Dialogue should be practical before it becomes mythic. The Keeper remembers loops because the mantle preserves the First Concord's mortal witness.

## Alden

Alden is a young Nordian Warrior and the Last Customer. He is brave, underprepared, and sincere rather than uniquely chosen. His death establishes that Contribution survives material failure. His victory makes him a Nordian public symbol and distant correspondent; he does not replace the Keeper or become the permanent player avatar. In later loops, Keeper foreknowledge gives him a stronger prepared start.

## Brina Ashdown

Brina knew Alden's border community and validates that his story belongs to people rather than propaganda. She begins near Nordia's destroy doctrine but values protection over glory. Her dramatic function is to make direct action persuasive without making it unquestionably correct.

## Eamon Vale

Eamon is a Zarabian seal-scholar barred from an archive for copying forbidden marginalia. He argues that striking a prisoner is not the same as repairing a prison. His curiosity must create risks as well as answers. He is the first character to recognize synchronized surges as one failing network.

## Mayor Corvin Rook

Corvin is the civic witness for debt, roads, refugees, and ordinary opinion. He never transfers ownership of the Hearth. He measures mythic decisions by whether townspeople can eat, travel, and survive them.

## Mara Vell

Mara is retired from current canon as a separate owner or antagonist. The Keeper portrait still uses a legacy asset path containing `tavern_mara`; that filename is not story identity. Old save fields for the former-owner encounter remain migration-only and cannot trigger.
