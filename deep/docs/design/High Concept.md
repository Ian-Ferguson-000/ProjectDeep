# Eros - High Concept

## Pitch
Eros is a tavern-management action roguelike about the Keeper of an ancient sanctuary at the crossroads of six rival nations. You recruit, equip, and sustain the adventurers who suppress failing divine prisons—and, when the world finally burns, you are the one who remembers enough to try again.

## Player Fantasy
You are not the hero. You are the person who makes heroes possible. The Hearth is your business, sanctuary, armory, and neutral ground. You decide whom to trust, what scarce supplies to risk, which unstable prison must be suppressed, and whether rival adventurers can survive the road together. During expeditions you directly control the people you prepared.

## Core Hook
Expedition failure can still leave Contribution: mapped danger, witnessed sacrifice, faction standing, and knowledge that helps the next company. The larger campaign begins when the Hearth itself is destroyed and its forgotten failsafe returns the Keeper to the morning the first Nordian adventurer entered.

## First Demo Promise
The first playable demo should prove the main loop:

1. Begin as the Keeper of the nearly empty Hearth.
2. Sponsor Alden, a young Nordian Warrior, with the last viable loadout.
3. Enter the Briarway and learn the expedition controls.
4. Fight, loot, map danger, and survive—or leave useful Contribution behind.
5. Resolve Alden's victory or death without changing the player's Keeper identity.
6. Receive Brina of Nordia and Eamon of Zarabia, whose conflicting beliefs begin the larger campaign.

## Tone
The game begins warm, grounded, and a little funny, then becomes darker and stranger the deeper the party descends. The forest should feel like the first step away from safety: readable, tactical, and dangerous without becoming overwhelming.

## Current Demo Scope
The active game is a persistent single-player, 2D, top-down roster campaign with six classes, real-time Slasher expeditions, eight dungeon destinations, permadeath, merchants, and tavern meta-progression. Structured nations, Contribution, and the Keeper-centered opening are the first implemented narrative foundation. Pantheons, dungeon pressure, geopolitical collapse, and the time-loop reset remain staged work described in `story/NarrativeAdaptationRoadmap.md`.
