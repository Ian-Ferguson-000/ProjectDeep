# Equipment and Inventory

**Purpose:** Define item ownership, storage, durability, assignment, and generation.  
**Audience:** Item designers and engineers.  
**Owner:** Systems/item design.  
**Status:** Canonical requirements.  
**Authority:** Item lifecycle and ownership.

Rarity controls affix complexity; item level controls numeric magnitude. Tavern-purchased and crafted equipment is tavern property and may be assigned among available adventurers. Personal quest items remain character-bound unless their explicit death/retirement policy creates an heirloom, recovery objective, or return to the quest giver.

Each expedition has bounded pack capacity. Equipped items do not consume pack slots; materials stack by type. At capacity, acquisition requires replacement, consumption, or abandonment. Tavern storage, repair, crafting, identification, curse handling, and stock quality expand through facilities and specialists.

Durability loss must be predictable by event category, never silently destroy equipped gear, and resolve before assignment at settlement. Item definitions contain shared identity/effects plus explicit Strategy or Slasher implementations only where timing requires them.
