# Items, Affixes, and Traits

**Purpose:** Define reusable item and adventurer-modifier content.  
**Audience:** Systems/content designers and engineers.  
**Owner:** Item/systems design.  
**Status:** Active migration.  
**Authority:** Item, affix, and trait authoring.

Definitions use stable IDs, tags, conditions, reusable effects, player-facing rules text, rarity/level constraints, incompatibilities, and optional mode implementations. Items must not maintain separate identity maps for Strategy and Slasher. Traits include reveal conditions and balanced benefits/costs; generated recruits may not receive combinations rejected by compatibility rules.
