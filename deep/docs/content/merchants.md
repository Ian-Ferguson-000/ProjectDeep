# Merchants

**Purpose:** Define merchant ownership and progression.  
**Audience:** Economy, content, narrative, and UI teams.  
**Owner:** Content/economy design.  
**Status:** Active.  
**Authority:** Demo merchant roster.

The demo has exactly six merchants: Mara Vell at the tavern and one merchant associated with each regular dungeon—Thistle Fen, Orin Cinder, Sister Caldris, Neris Vale, and Veyra Coil. Secret dungeons have no merchant.

Merchant definitions declare location, recruitment rule, stock tables, Favor thresholds, specialties, dialogue/event pools, facility requirement, and asset references. Gold purchases current stock; Favor normally unlocks tiers without being consumed.
