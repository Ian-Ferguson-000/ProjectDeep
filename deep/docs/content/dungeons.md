# Dungeons and Regions

**Purpose:** Define the demo destination graph and scalable destination contract.  
**Audience:** Dungeon, narrative, encounter, and art teams.  
**Owner:** Dungeon design.  
**Status:** Active.  
**Authority:** Demo destinations and unlock relationships.

| Destination | Type | Unlock | Major reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Verdant Forest | Regular | Initial | Farmstead, Crypt, larger parties, Tank/Rogue by mode |
| Ashen Farmstead | Regular | Forest clear | Healer, Grove clue |
| Stone Crypt | Regular | Forest clear | Summoner, Archive clue |
| Sunken Mine | Regular | Farmstead or Crypt | Mineral/armor access |
| Ember Foundry | Regular | Farmstead and Crypt | High-rarity forge access, Archive clue |
| Moonlit Grove | Secret | Forest/Farmstead clues plus research | Nature/fate relics |
| Abyssal Archive | Secret | Crypt/Foundry clues plus research | Arcane/void relics |

Each definition declares region, destination type, modes, party cap, depth/floor model, generator, objectives, encounters, threat/reward curves, extraction rule, merchant, clues, rewards, unlock expression, visuals, audio, and fallback layout. Hell and future regions extend this catalog; they do not replace the current demo graph.
