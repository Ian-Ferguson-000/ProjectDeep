GAME LOOP


Start Game - 

Enter Tavern

Adventurer enters

Adventurer Leaves and goes on expedition

Adventurer Dies on Tutorial
->
We return to tavern after death screen as the tavern keeper - we then get the loop of making adventurers stronger to then get more money in future etc etc

Second pair of adventurers arrive. Mage and Fighter. You feed them and send them out on an adventure telling them to return here when they are done to pay there TAB

TAB -  System in game of how much you spent on an adventurer this corelates to certain bonuses if you spend more they might have a better gold % chance this is hidden feature and not viewable to Gamer

Adventurer Survives on Tutorial
->
The tavern went backrupt and the tavern keeper has left town you return to find the town mayor in the tavern complaining about the lack of bar in the village. You are then offered it in return for the gold you found after beating the boss -  tavern starts with a letter hidden in the house saying the tavern keeper will return to take what is his. sets up a fight against the tavern keeper later down the line as a secret easter egg

Regardless of Live or Die tutorial

The game loop then begins

Adventurers come to the bar before starting the adventure you can recruit them by offering them a room for the night. you can dismiss them by saying the rooms are booked and they must leave.

adventurers in the tavern can have multiple scenarios that can show certain skills ( We can see a certain amount early) 
i.e we can see a fighter style come in but his strength is hidden. over the course of an evening of talking to adventurers and making food and ale he challenges you to an arm wrestling match 
You lose you lose money but find out hes stronger than your strength stat
you win you get money but find out hes weaker than your strength stat
a tie noone wins but you get his strength stat

As you continue to use the available adventurers and start winning runs we can start upgrading the tavern

The tavern upgrades are consisting of Rooms - Food tiers - Drink Tiers - Armory - Blacksmith (Character who works with you from there shop) and a magician specialising in magic items
the more we upgrade the tavern the higher skill and quality we get in terms of adventurer
Which is progression in stats alone and gives a nice gambling affect

Onto the levels and such

Levels are Forest - Crypt - Hell

you complete one you move down or go home

this creates the loop of im feeling strong and can do this or fuck itll be tough maybe best to save what i got

What the adventurer can bring back is gold - Items and equipment.
