# New Beginnings - Starter Demo GDD

## High Concept
- Working title: Eros
- Genre: Turn-based tabletop roguelike
- Platform: PC
- Target audience: Roguelike players, tabletop RPG players, and D&D-inspired fantasy fans
- One-sentence pitch: A turn-based roguelike dungeon crawler where every death returns you to the tavern to bargain, learn, choose new gear, and descend again.

## Vision Statement
Each run should feel like a compact tabletop adventure: clear tactical choices, dangerous surprises, useful loot, and a return to the tavern that makes the next descent feel tempting.

## Design Pillars
1. Roguelike momentum: quick runs, meaningful risk, death as part of the loop.
2. Tabletop fantasy: grid movement, readable tokens, dice-inspired combat, secrets, traps, and gear identity.
3. Tavern ritual: every run begins and ends in the same memorable place.

## Core Player Fantasy
The player is a Fighter taking their first contract from a mysterious bartender. They choose a fighting style, step into a living forest dungeon, and survive through positioning, equipment identity, and tactical choices.

## Core Loop
1. Begin in the tavern hub.
2. Talk to the bartender and select starter gear.
3. Enter the forest floor.
4. Explore rooms and paths on a grid.
5. Fight enemies, avoid traps, break props, find loot, and discover one hidden cache.
6. Escape through the exit door or die.
7. Return to the tavern with a short outcome message.

## First Playable Scope
Must-have:
- Tavern hub.
- One playable Fighter.
- Three starting gear kits.
- One seeded forest dungeon with a guaranteed path to the exit.
- Simple turn-based combat.
- Basic loot, chest, trap, destructible props, and one secret.
- Death and completion return to the tavern.

Future scope:
- Multiple classes.
- Multiplayer.
- Daily challenges.
- Persistent campaign progression.
- More biomes such as cave, hell, and deeper unknown floors.
- Full procedural campaign generation.

## Success Criteria
The demo succeeds when a player can understand the loop without explanation: pick gear, leave the tavern, navigate the forest board, use a weapon identity, get rewarded or punished, and come back wanting another run.

## Art and Audio Direction
- Visual style: clean 2D tabletop board with tokens, icons, clear silhouettes, and simple effects.
- Camera: top-down.
- UI: readable, compact, and functional.
- Audio: simple tavern ambience, forest ambience, hits, pickups, and transition stingers when available.
