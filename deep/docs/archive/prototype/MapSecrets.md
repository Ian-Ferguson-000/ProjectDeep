# Map Secrets

Secrets should reward attention without blocking progression. The starter demo uses one simple secret so the mechanic is visible without needing a full perception system.

## Demo Secret: Hidden Cache
- Appears as a suspicious patch of ground in the forest.
- Can be discovered by interacting while adjacent.
- Rewards gold and a potion.
- Does not require a key.

## Future Secret Types
- Magic doors: easier for high Intelligence or magic-focused classes.
- Trap doors: easier for Fighters, Paladins, or high Strength characters to notice and force open.
- Hidden caches: easier for Rogues, Rangers, or high Wisdom characters.
- Biome secrets: dwarves may notice cave seams more easily; elves may notice forest traces more easily.

## Sanity Idea
A future sanity meter could affect how reliable secret detection feels. For the starter demo, sanity is out of scope.
