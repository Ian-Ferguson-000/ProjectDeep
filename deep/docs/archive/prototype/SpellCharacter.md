# Mage spells

The starter demo offers three Mage spell books. Each book should change how the player thinks about positioning.

## Magic Missile and Shield
- Damage: 1
- Defense: can block attacks with limited block stacks
- Special: Force Blast
- Force blast sends out a large projectile that pushes the target back and damages enemies in an aoe.

## Fireball and Fire Shield
- Damage: 3
- Defense: does 1 damage on recieving hit
- Special: Flamethrower
- Ignites the ground in a line in front of the player

## Lightning bolt and Flash Step
- Damage: 2
- Defense: teleports backwards 2 spaces
- Special: Shockwave
- Stuns adjacent enemies for a short duration

## Demo Necklace
Veteran Necklace is a future non-weapon gear idea. It grants Battle Hardened, preventing critical hits from the front. It is out of scope for the starter demo.

## Future Boss Rewards
- Knight: tank and shield specialist.
- Berserker: two-handed damage and rage.
- Duelist: counterattacks and mobility.
- Warlord: ally buffs and battlecry effects.
