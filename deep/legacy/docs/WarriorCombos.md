# Warrior combo catalogue

There are **36 playable Warrior combos: six per kit**. Open the training hall, choose a Warrior weapon, and enable **Combos [C]**. The optional panel selects the matching recipe set and shows named steps, remaining time, the next input, payoff, failures, and successes. See [Warrior builds](WarriorBuilds.md) for the four abilities and their tactical constraints.

**B** = Basic; **S** = Special; **D** = Defensive; **M** = Movement. Click, Space, Shift-click, and right-click are their mouse/keyboard bindings. Controller equivalents use the same action stream. New sequences have **2.5 seconds per accepted step**; the standard's existing Flowing Assault retains its shorter 0.75s window. Rejections preserve progress, while wrong successful actions reset or restart a matching prefix. Each named kit has its own six recipes; standard recipes are not inherited by alternatives.

The finishing ability still occurs normally. The new payoff is additional unless it explicitly modifies that pending ability. Costs and cooldowns remain those of each slot. All damage below is **physical** and is expressed as a multiplier of attack power before current progression/item modifiers; values are provisional. Secondary hits never grant Stamina, trigger item hit effects, or echo recursively. No combo moves bosses, bypasses shields/caps, heals, or resurrects. Support recipients are living, visible, actively deployed players on the same combat floor; benched/dead actors are excluded.

Use single clicks for recipe practice. Held Basic inserts repeated inputs. Leave a clear placement area for guards/backsteps and a clear path for tether movement. A conditional recipe can complete without ideal setup, but its mark/field-dependent payoff requires the described setup. Resource comes from the preceding connecting attacks rather than automatic combo refunds.

## Conditional payoffs and safeguards

Red Sentence modifies the finishing delayed cleave, while Perfect Measure empowers one future Crossing Blades within two seconds; neither overrides its cost. Long Watch and Mobile Redoubt modify the caster's existing lane, and Second Rank provides one additional independently timed single-use lane. Unbroken Standard can add its extra two seconds **once per planting**, so repeated combos cannot keep one banner alive forever. Banner relocation does not replant or refresh it. Captain's Oath selects its ally near the banner; Vanguard Crash selects one near the landing; Rally March reaches eligible allies near the Warrior. All support bonuses have finite duration and use nonstacking shared buffs.

Last Word and Phantom Flurry snapshot departure geometry; they do not follow or home onto targets. Anchor Crash uses the pre-movement tether point, and Linked Weights strikes at most two distinct additional nearby targets with a clear chain path. Shared barrier replacement and source cleanup prevent persistent shields across build swaps. Bosses receive damage but no combo-only hard stagger, slow, or forced movement.

The complete action suite exercises all 36 recipes with a three-Stamina budget, verifies every new executor branch, and checks that the practice panel receives completion feedback. The targeted tests verify representative payloads, timing, resource restrictions, ownership, and reset behavior. Final encounter balance and dedicated weapon art/audio remain playtesting and production decisions.
