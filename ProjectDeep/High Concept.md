## 1. High Concept
- Working title: Eros
- Genre: Rougelike Tabletop
- Platforms: PC
- Target audience: Roguelike and DND
- One-sentence pitch: A turn-based roguelike dungeon crawler where every death sends you back to the tavern to speak with the enigmatic bartender, bargain for new powers, and descend once more into a grand, dangerous world of Dungeons & Dragons-inspired peril, tactical combat, and relentless replayability.

Eros is a brutal yet evocative adventure in which each run begins at the tavern—your sanctuary, your crossroads, and the place where fate is whispered into your ear. From there, you journey into ever-changing dungeons filled with mythic monsters, deadly traps, and sacred relics, fighting in a tactical, turn-based style inspired by the best of tabletop fantasy and modern roguelikes. Every death is not a defeat, but a new beginning; every return to the tavern brings fresh stories, new bargains, and greater knowledge of the horrors lurking below. With a rich, painterly fantasy aesthetic and the addictive momentum of a roguelike, Eros blends the soul of classic dungeon exploration with the strategic depth of a modern fantasy RPG.
