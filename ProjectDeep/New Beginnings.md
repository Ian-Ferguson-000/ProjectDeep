# New Beginnings - Game Design Document

## 1. High Concept
- Working title: Eros
- Genre: Rougelike Tabletop
- Platforms: PC
- Target audience: Roguelike and DND
- One-sentence pitch: 

## 2. Vision Statement
Every run needs to be immersive for your character each time and challenging enough to bring you back

## 3. Design Pillars
1. Pillar 1: Roguelike aspects
2. Pillar 2: Dungeons and Dragons world building
3. Pillar 3: Tabletop with friends

## 4. Core Player Fantasy
Who the player is, what they can do, and why it feels compelling.

## 5. Core Loop
### Loop Summary
Crawling through dungeons with your party to dive deeper and deeper from forest to cave to hell to depths unknown

### Loop Steps
1. Step 1: Start in forest get basic randomised gear
2. Step 2: Go deeper get better gear to go deeper
3. Step 3: Unknown
4. Step 4: Profit?

## 6. Game Structure
- Session length: Each Floor (i.e Forest/Cave) slowly would take 30 minutes? can be stopped at any point
- Game modes: Single player. Daily Challenges. Multiplayer
- Save/progression model: Single player is campaign based. Multiplayer is randomised runs

## 7. Systems Overview
### Movement
Click and pick

### Combat or Challenge
Combat is dnd turn based with dice rolls for actions etc

### Economy and Resources
Balancing gold to spend in special shops your main resource is health

### Progression
Go deeper get better. Completing a run allows you to go deeper on the next. 

## 8. World and Narrative Basics
- Setting: Fantasy
- Tone: Light to Dark
- Story premise: Tavern quest leads to discovery
- Player objective: Fight the sun

## 9. Art and Audio Direction
- Visual style keywords: 2d Top down basic tabletop
- Camera perspective: Top Down
- Audio style keywords: Simple audio
- UI style keywords: Simple

## 10. Scope and Production Guardrails
- Must-have features for first playable: One playable class 2 playable floors and procedurally generated levels
- Nice-to-have features: Multiple different items that can be equipped
- Out of scope for now: Multiple classes

## 11. Milestones
- Prototype target date: 1/10/2026
- First playable target date: 1/09/2026
